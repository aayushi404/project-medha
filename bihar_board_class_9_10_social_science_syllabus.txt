UNIT 1: HISTORY (इतिहास : इतिहास की दुनिया भाग - 1) [20 Marks]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Chapter 1: Geographical Discoveries (भौगोलिक खोजें)
  - Introduction to Geographical Discoveries (भौगोलिक खोजें - परिचय)
  - Background and Causes of Geographical Discoveries (भौगोलिक खोजों की पृष्ठभूमि):
    * Fall of Constantinople (1453) and trade route blockades
    * Advances in navigation, compass, astrolabe, shipbuilding, and cartography
    * Role of Portugal and Spain (Bartolomeu Dias, Vasco da Gama, Christopher Columbus, Ferdinand Magellan)
  - Impact and Consequences of Geographical Discoveries (भौगोलिक खोजों के परिणाम):
    * Expansion of global maritime trade and rise of Atlantic commercial centers
    * Colonialism, slave trade, commercial revolution, and cultural exchange

Chapter 2: American War of Independence (अमेरिकी स्वतंत्रता संग्राम)
  - Historical Background of the 13 British Colonies in North America
  - Causes of the American War of Independence (स्वतंत्रता संग्राम के कारण):
    * Mercantilist policies and unfair taxation (Stamp Act, Sugar Act, Tea Act)
    * Boston Tea Party (1773) and "No Taxation Without Representation"
    * Intellectual awakening and role of writers (Thomas Paine, John Locke, Thomas Jefferson)
    * Declaration of Independence (4 July 1776) and leadership of George Washington
  - Impact of Industrialization (औद्योगीकरण का प्रभाव)
  - Causes of British Failure in the War (इंग्लैण्ड की असफलता के कारण)
  - Results and Global Impact of the American Revolution (परिणाम एवं प्रभाव)

Chapter 3: The French Revolution (फ्रांस की क्रान्ति - 1789)
  - Political Causes (राजनैतिक कारण): Autocratic monarchy of Louis XVI and Bourbon dynasty, administrative centralization, and corruption
  - Social Causes (सामाजिक कारण): Division of French society into Three Estates (Clergy, Nobility, and Commoners/Middle Class & Peasants)
  - Economic Causes (आर्थिक कारण): Empty royal treasury, war debts, regressive tax burden (Tithe and Taille), and subsistence crisis
  - Military Causes (सैनिक कारण): Dissatisfaction and poor conditions in the army
  - Personal and Religious Causes (व्यक्तिगत एवं धार्मिक कारण): Religious intolerance and privileges of the higher clergy
  - Intellectual Causes (बौद्धिक कारण): Role of Enlightenment philosophers (Montesquieu, Voltaire, Rousseau, Diderot)
  - Influence of External Events (विदेशी घटनाओं का प्रभाव): Impact of the American Revolution and English constitutionalism
  - Course and Chronology of the Revolution (क्रांति का घटनाक्रम): Estates-General, Tennis Court Oath, Fall of the Bastille (14 July 1789), Declaration of the Rights of Man and Citizen
  - The Reign of Terror (आतंक का राज्य): Jacobins, Robespierre, and the Guillotine
  - Nature of the Revolution (क्रांति का स्वरूप)
  - Outcomes and Legacy of the Revolution (क्रांति के परिणाम): Abolition of feudalism, rise of liberty, equality, fraternity, and secularism
  - Impact of the French Revolution on Other Countries (अन्य देशों पर क्रांति का प्रभाव)

Chapter 4: History of the World Wars (विश्वयुद्धों का इतिहास)
  - First World War (प्रथम विश्वयुद्ध: 1914-1918):
    * Causes of World War I (प्रथम विश्वयुद्ध के कारण): Secret alliances, militarism, imperialism, narrow nationalism, and the Sarajevo assassination
    * Outbreak and Course of the War (युद्ध की शुरुआत एवं घटनाक्रम): Allied Powers vs. Central Powers, trench warfare, entry of the USA, Russian withdrawal
    * End of the War and Peace Treaties (युद्ध का अंत और शांति-संधियाँ): Paris Peace Conference (1919)
    * Treaty of Versailles (वर्साय की संधि): Territorial, military, and economic clauses imposed on Germany
    * Consequences of the War and Peace Treaties (युद्ध और शांति संधियों के परिणाम)
    * Rise of a New Political Order (नयी राजनीतिक व्यवस्था का उदय)
  - Second World War (द्वितीय विश्वयुद्ध: 1939-1945):
    * Preparation of the Background for World War II (द्वितीय विश्वयुद्ध की पृष्ठभूमि तैयार)
    * Causes of World War II (द्वितीय विश्वयुद्ध के कारण):
      - Anomalies and humiliation of the Treaty of Versailles (वर्साय संधि की विसंगतियाँ)
      - Breach of international commitments (वचन विमुखता)
      - Spanish Civil War and internal conflicts (गृहयुद्ध)
      - Arms race and militarization (हथियारबंदी)
      - Failure of the League of Nations (राष्ट्रसंघ की असफलता)
      - The Great Depression of 1929 (विश्वव्यापी आर्थिक मंदी)
      - Rise of Fascism and Nazism: Mussolini and Hitler (नवीन विचारधाराओं - हिटलर एवं मुसोलिनी का उदय)
      - Policy of Appeasement by Britain and France (तुष्टिकरण की नीति)
    * Outbreak and Chronology of World War II (युद्ध का आरंभ और घटनाक्रम): German invasion of Poland (1939), Axis vs. Allies, Pearl Harbor, Stalingrad, Hiroshima & Nagasaki
    * Consequences and Impact of World War II (द्वितीय विश्वयुद्ध के परिणाम): End of European hegemony, Cold War bipolarity, decolonization, and birth of the UNO

Chapter 5: Rise of Nazism in Germany (जर्मनी में नाजीवाद का उदय)
  - Weimar Republic and Post-WWI Crisis in Germany
  - Causes of the Rise of Adolf Hitler (हिटलर के उत्कर्ष के कारण): Economic crisis, treaty humiliation, political instability, fear of communism, charismatic propaganda
  - Hitler and His Domestic Actions (हिटलर एवं उसके कार्य): Dictatorship, suppression of opposition, secret police (Gestapo), economic reconstruction
  - Nazi Ideology and Philosophy (नाजीवादी दर्शन): Racial supremacy (Nordic Aryan theory), anti-Semitism, Holocaust, Lebensraum (living space), total state control
  - Hitler's Foreign Policy (हिटलर की विदेश नीति): Withdrawal from League of Nations, Rhineland remilitarization, Rome-Berlin-Tokyo Axis, Munich Pact, invasion of Poland
  - Domestic and Global Impact of Nazism (नाजीवाद का प्रभाव)

Chapter 6: Forest/Tribal Society and Colonialism (वन्य-समाज और उपनिवेशवाद / आदिवासी समाज और उपनिवेशवाद)
  - Introduction to Forest and Tribal Societies (भूमिका)
  - Structure of Tribal Society (वन्य समाज का जीवन):
    * Political Life of Tribals (राजनैतिक जीवन)
    * Social Life and Customs (सामाजिक जीवन)
    * Economic Life: Shifting cultivation (Jhum), hunting, gathering, pastoralism (आर्थिक जीवन)
    * Religious Life and Beliefs (धार्मिक जीवन)
  - Colonial Forest Laws, Commercial Forestry, and Exploitation by Dikus (Moneylenders, Traders, British Officials)
  - Major Tribal Revolts Against Colonialism (प्रमुख जनजातीय विद्रोह):
    * Paharia Revolt (पहाड़िया विद्रोह)
    * Tamar Revolt (तमार विद्रोह)
    * Chero Revolt (चेरो विद्रोह)
    * Chuar Revolt (चुआर विद्रोह)
    * 'Ho' Revolt ('हो' विद्रोह)
    * Kol Mutiny / Revolt (कोल विद्रोह - 1831-32)
    * Bhumij Revolt (भूमिज विद्रोह)
    * Santhal Hul / Rebellion (संथाल विद्रोह - 1855-56: Sidhu and Kanhu)
    * Munda Ulgulan / Rebellion (मुंडा विद्रोह - 1899-1900: Birsa Munda)
    * Khond Revolt (कंध विद्रोह - Chakra Bisoi)

Chapter 7: Efforts for World Peace (विश्वशांति के प्रयास)
  - The League of Nations (राष्ट्रसंघ - League of Nations):
    * Establishment (1920) and Objectives of the League of Nations (राष्ट्रसंघ के उद्देश्य)
    * Member Nations of the League (राष्ट्रसंघ के सदस्य राष्ट्र)
    * Principal Organs of the League of Nations (राष्ट्रसंघ के अंग): Assembly, Council, Secretariat, Permanent Court of International Justice, ILO
    * Peace Efforts by the League of Nations (राष्ट्रसंघ द्वारा शांति के प्रयास)
    * Role in Resolving International Disputes (अंतर्राष्ट्रीय विवादों को सुलझाने में भूमिका)
    * Achievements in Non-Political / Social and Economic Fields (अन्य क्षेत्रों में राष्ट्रसंघ की सफलता)
    * Causes of the Failure of the League of Nations (राष्ट्रसंघ की असफलता के कारण)
  - The United Nations Organization (संयुक्त राष्ट्रसंघ - U.N.O.):
    * Background of Establishment and San Francisco Conference, 1945 (संयुक्त राष्ट्रसंघ के स्थापना की पृष्ठभूमि और सेनफ्रांसिस्को सम्मेलन)
    * Aims and Objectives of the United Nations (संयुक्त राष्ट्रसंघ के लक्ष्य एवं उद्देश्य)
    * Principles of the United Nations (संयुक्त राष्ट्रसंघ के सिद्धांत)
    * Six Principal Organs of the UN (संयुक्त राष्ट्रसंघ के अंग): General Assembly, Security Council, Economic and Social Council (ECOSOC), Trusteeship Council, International Court of Justice (ICJ), Secretariat
    * Specialized Agencies (UNESCO, UNICEF, WHO, FAO, IMF, World Bank)
    * Successes and Failures of the UN (संयुक्त राष्ट्रसंघ की सफलता एवं असफलताएँ)
    * Causes of Limitations/Failures in Peacekeeping (असफलता के कारण)

Chapter 8: Agriculture and Agrarian Society (कृषि और खेतिहर समाज)
  - Introduction to Agrarian History (भूमिका)
  - Cropping Seasons and Crops of Bihar (बिहार की फसलें):
    * Bhadai Crops (भदई फसलें - Autumn harvest: maize, paddy, jute)
    * Agahani Crops (अगहनी फसलें - Winter harvest: winter paddy, sugarcane)
    * Rabi Crops (रबी फसलें - Spring harvest: wheat, gram, mustard, barley, pulses)
    * Garma Crops (गरमा फसलें - Summer harvest: summer paddy, moong, vegetables, fruits)
  - Classification of Crops Based on Nature (फसलों का उनकी प्रकृति के आधार पर वर्गीकरण)
  - History of Different Farming Systems (विभिन्न प्रकार की खेतियों का इतिहास)
  - Importance of Agriculture in the Rural Economy (ग्रामीण अर्थव्यवस्था में इसकी जरूरत)
  - Transformations in the Contemporary Rural Economy (वर्तमान समय में ग्रामीण अर्थव्यवस्था में परिवर्तन)
  - Major Commercial / Cash Crops Grown in Bihar (बिहार में होनेवाली प्रमुख व्यावसायिक फसलें)
  - Scientific Approach and Modernization in Agriculture (कृषि में वैज्ञानिक दृष्टिकोण)

UNIT 2: GEOGRAPHY (भूगोल : भारत : भूमि एवं लोग - खण्ड 'क') [20 Marks]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Chapter 1: Location and Extent of India (स्थिति एवं विस्तार)
  - Geographical Location of India in the Northern and Eastern Hemispheres (स्थिति)
  - Size and Area of India: 3.28 million sq. km, 7th largest in the world (आकार)
  - Land Frontier and Coastline of India: 15,200 km land border; 7,516.6 km coastline including islands (भारत की स्थल एवं तटीय सीमा रेखा)
  - Implications of Latitudinal and Longitudinal Extent (अक्षांशीय एवं देशांतरीय विस्तार का प्रभाव):
    * Tropic of Cancer (23°30' N) passing through 8 states
    * Indian Standard Meridian (82°30' E passing through Mirzapur) and 2-hour time lag between Arunachal Pradesh and Gujarat
  - India and the World: Strategic central location at the head of the Indian Ocean, trans-Indian Ocean routes (भारत तथा विश्व)
  - India's Neighbouring Countries: Land and maritime neighbours across SAARC region (भारत के पड़ोसी देश)

Chapter 2: Physical Features: Structure and Relief (भौतिक स्वरूप : संरचना एवं उच्चावच)
  - Introduction to Geological Structure and Plate Tectonics: Gondwanaland, Tethys Sea, and Himalayan uplift (भूमिका)
  - Major Physiographic Divisions of India (भौतिक विभाग):
    * The Himalayan Mountain Range (हिमालय पर्वत श्रेणी): Trans-Himalayas (Karakoram, Ladakh, Zaskar), Greater Himalayas (Himadri), Lesser Himalayas (Himachal), and Outer Himalayas (Shiwaliks); Regional divisions (Punjab, Kumaon, Nepal, Assam Himalayas) and Purvanchal Hills
    * The Great Northern Plains (उत्तर का विशाल मैदान): Bhabar, Terai, Bhangar, and Khadar; Indus, Ganga, and Brahmaputra plains (including Bihar plain divisions)
    * The Peninsular Plateau (दक्षिण का पठार): Central Highlands (Malwa, Bundelkhand, Chotanagpur) and Deccan Plateau; Western Ghats (Sahyadri) and Eastern Ghats
    * The Coastal Plains (तटीय मैदान): Western Coastal Plain (Konkan, Kannad, Malabar) and Eastern Coastal Plain (Northern Circar, Coromandel)
    * The Indian Desert (भारतीय मरुस्थल - थार मरुस्थल): Arid topography, Barchans, Luni river
    * The Island Groups (द्वीप समूह): Lakshadweep Islands (coral origin) and Andaman & Nicobar Islands (submarine mountain peaks)

Chapter 3: Drainage System (अपवाह स्वरूप)
  - Introduction to Drainage Basins, Watersheds, and Drainage Patterns (परिचय)
  - Himalayan River Systems vs. Peninsular River Systems (हिमालय की नदियाँ एवं प्रायद्वीपीय नदियाँ)
  - Major Rivers of India (भारत की प्रमुख नदियाँ):
    * Indus River System (सिंधु नदी तंत्र): Indus, Jhelum, Chenab, Ravi, Beas, Sutlej
    * Ganga River System (गंगा नदी तंत्र): Bhagirathi, Alaknanda, Yamuna (यमुना नदी), Ghaghara (घाघरा), Gandak (गंडक नदी), Kosi (कोसी नदी - Sorrow of Bihar), Son, Damodar
    * Brahmaputra River System (ब्रह्मपुत्र नदी तंत्र): Tsangpo, Dihang, Jamuna, Majuli island
    * Peninsular Rivers: Narmada (नर्मदा नदी), Tapi (तापी नदी), Mahanadi (महानदी), Godavari (गोदावरी नदी - Dakshin Ganga), Krishna (कृष्णा नदी), Kaveri (कावेरी नदी)
  - Lakes and Reservoirs: Types of Lakes Based on Formation — Tectonic, Ox-bow (Kawar Lake in Begusarai, Bihar), Lagoon (Chilika, Pulicat), Glacial, Salt-water (Sambhar), and Man-made Lakes (जलाशय/झील निर्माण की दृष्टि से झीलों के प्रकार)
  - Rivers as the Lifeline of Human Civilization (मानव सभ्यता की जीवन रेखा के रूप में नदियाँ)
  - River Water Pollution, Conservation, and Vegetation Growth (प्रदूषण, संरक्षण, वनस्पति का विकास)
  - National River Conservation Plan (NRCP) and Ganga Action Plan / Namami Gange (राष्ट्रीय नदी संरक्षण योजना)
  - Creation of National Water Harvesting Centers (राष्ट्रीय जल-संग्रहण केन्द्र का निर्माण)
  - Impact of Drainage System on Human Life and Economy (मानव जीवन पर अपवाह तंत्र का प्रभाव)

Chapter 4: Climate (जलवायु)
  - Introduction: Difference between Weather and Climate; Monsoon type of climate in India (भूमिका)
  - Factors Affecting India's Climate (भारतीय जलवायु को प्रभावित करने वाले कारक): Latitude, Altitude, Pressure and Wind Systems, Distance from the Sea, Ocean Currents (El Nino/La Nina), and Relief Features
  - The Indian Monsoon and Its Mechanism (भारतीय मॉनसून): ITCZ shift, differential heating of land and sea, Tibetan Plateau heating, Jet Streams (Westerly and Tropical Easterly), Southern Oscillation
  - Nature and Characteristics of the Indian Monsoon: Onset, burst, breaks, and variability (मॉनसून का स्वभाव)
  - Rainfall and Its Types: Orographic, Convectional, and Cyclonic Rainfall; Spatial distribution of rainfall in India (वर्षा एवं उसके प्रकार)
  - Cycle of Seasons in India (ऋतुएँ):
    * Cold Weather Season / Winter (Mid-November to February)
    * Hot Weather Season / Summer (March to May — Loo, Kaal Baisakhi, Mango Showers)
    * Advancing Monsoon / Rainy Season (June to September — Arabian Sea and Bay of Bengal branches)
    * Retreating Monsoon / Transition Season (लौटते मॉनसून का मौसम — October Heat, cyclonic rainfall on Tamil Nadu coast)
  - Impact of Climate and Monsoon on Human Life and Agriculture (मानव जीवन पर प्रभाव)

Chapter 5: Natural Vegetation and Wildlife (प्राकृतिक वनस्पति एवं वन्य प्राणी)
  - Introduction: Virgin vegetation, endemic vs. exotic species, flora and fauna (भूमिका)
  - Causes of Diversity in Flora and Fauna in India (भारत में वनस्पति एवं वन्य प्राणी में विविधता के कारण): Relief (land, soil) and Climate (temperature, photoperiod, precipitation)
  - Ecosystem and Biome Balance (पारिस्थितिक तंत्र)
  - Types of Natural Vegetation in India (वनस्पति के प्रकार):
    * Tropical Evergreen Forests (उष्णकटिबंधीय सदाबहार वन)
    * Tropical Deciduous / Monsoon Forests — Moist and Dry (उष्णकटिबंधीय पर्णपाती या पतझड़ वन)
    * Tropical Thorn Forests and Scrubs (कँटीले वन तथा झाड़ियाँ)
    * Montane / Mountain Forests (पर्वतीय वन)
    * Mangrove / Tidal Forests — Sundarbans (मैंग्रोव या ज्वारीय वन)

  - India as a Global Service Provider: BPO, KPO, Call Centers, IT and Software Exports (Bengaluru, Pune, Hyderabad, Noida) (भारत विश्व को सेवा प्रदाता के रूप में)
  - Basic Infrastructure for the Service Sector (सेवा क्षेत्र की बुनियादी सुविधाएँ):
    * (1) Economic Infrastructure (आर्थिक आधारभूत संरचनाएँ): Finance, Energy (Coal, Electricity, Oil, Gas), Transport (Rail, Road, Air, Water), and Communication
    * (2) Non-Economic / Social Infrastructure (गैर-आर्थिक आधारभूत संरचनाएँ): Education, Health, Housing, and Civic Services
  - Role of Education and Skill Development in the Service Sector (सेवा क्षेत्र में शिक्षा की भूमिका)

Chapter 6: Globalization (वैश्वीकरण)
  - Introduction and Meaning of Globalization (भूमिका, वैश्वीकरण क्या है ? - Integration of domestic economy with the world economy)

  - Role and Working Mechanism of Multinational Corporations (MNCs) in the Globalization Process (वैश्वीकरण की प्रक्रिया में बहुराष्ट्रीय कंपनियों की भूमिका, बहुराष्ट्रीय कंपनी की कार्य-प्रणाली)
  - Interlinking Production Across Countries and Integration of Markets by MNCs (बहुराष्ट्रीय कंपनियों द्वारा विश्व भर में उत्पादन को एक-दूसरे से जोड़ना, वैश्वीकरण एवं बाजारों का एकीकरण)
  - Factors That Have Enabled Globalization (वैश्वीकरण को संभव बनाने वाले कारक): Rapid improvements in transport and Information & Communication Technology (ICT), Liberalization of foreign trade and investment
  - World Trade Organization (WTO, est. 1995): Objectives, functions, and debates on fair globalization (विश्व व्यापार संगठन)
  - Why Globalization in India? Historical Perspective (Pre-1991 vs. Post-1991) (भारत में वैश्वीकरण क्यों ? वैश्वीकरण ऐतिहासिक परिवेश में - 1991 के पूर्व का वैश्वीकरण)
  - Economic Reforms of 1991 / New Economic Policy (LPG: Liberalization, Privatization, Globalization):
    * Meaning of Economic Reforms (सन् 1991 का आर्थिक सुधार, आर्थिक सुधारों का अर्थ)
    * Objectives and Main Features of the New Economic Policy (आर्थिक सुधारों अथवा नई आर्थिक नीति के उद्देश्य एवं मुख्य विशेषताएँ)
  - Impact of Globalization on Bihar: Positive Impacts and Negative Impacts (बिहार में वैश्वीकरण का प्रभाव : सकारात्मक प्रभाव, नकारात्मक प्रभाव)
  - Globalization and the Common Man / Small Producers & Workers (वैश्वीकरण एवं आम आदमी)

Chapter 7: Consumer Awareness and Protection (उपभोक्ता जागरण एवं संरक्षण)

  - Need for Consumer Awareness and Major Slogans ("Jago Grahak Jago") (उपभोक्ता जागरण)
  - Forms and Factors of Consumer Exploitation in the Marketplace: Underweighing, adulteration, high prices, duplicate goods, misleading advertisements, lack of safety devices (उपभोक्ता शोषण)

  - Consumer Complaint: What, Where, and How to File a Complaint? ('उपभोक्ता शिकायत' : क्या, कहाँ, कैसे ?)
  - Redressal of Broader Socio-Economic Exploitation: National & State Human Rights Commissions (मानवाधिकार आयोग) and Central & State Information Commissions / RTI 2005 (सूचना आयोग)

UNIT 5: DISASTER MANAGEMENT (आपदा प्रबंधन : भारत : संसाधन एवं उपयोग - खण्ड 'ख') [06 Marks]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Chapter 1: Natural Disasters: An Introduction (प्राकृतिक आपदा : एक परिचय)
  - Introduction to Disasters: Classification into Natural Disasters (Earthquake, Tsunami, Flood, Drought, Landslide, Cyclone, Hailstorm, Avalanche) and Man-Made Disasters (Terrorism, Riots, Fire, Rail/Road Accidents, Chemical/Biological/Nuclear disasters) (विषय प्रवेश)
  - Concept, Objectives, and Necessity of Natural Disaster Management (प्राकृतिक आपदा प्रबंधन)

Chapter 2: Natural Disasters and Management: Flood and Drought (प्राकृतिक आपदा एवं प्रबंधन : बाढ़ और सुखाड़)
  - Floods: Causes of Floods, Flood-Prone Regions in India and North Bihar (Kosi, Gandak, Bagmati, Kamla Balan, Burhi Gandak basins)
  - Case Reference: Bangladesh — Country of Floods (बांग्लादेश : बाढ़ का देश)
  - Flood Management (बाढ़ प्रबंधन):
    * Construction of Dams, Reservoirs, and Embankments (बाँध और तटबंध का निर्माण)
    * Alternative Flood Management: Interlinking of rivers, flood-resistant crops, elevated housing (वैकल्पिक प्रबंधन)
    * Early Warning and Forecasting Systems (पूर्व सूचना का प्रबंधन)
  - Drought and Its Management (सुखाड़ तथा इसका प्रबंधन):
    * Causes of Drought and Drought-Prone Regions in India and South Bihar (Gaya, Nawada, Jamui, Banka, Aurangabad, Munger) (भारत में सुखाड़ का क्षेत्र)
    * Long-term and Short-term Drought Management: Canal network, drip/sprinkler irrigation, dryland farming (सुखाड़ प्रबंधन)
    * What is Groundwater? Importance of Watershed Development and Rainwater Harvesting (भूमिगत जल क्या है, वर्षा जल संग्रहण)

Chapter 3: Natural Disasters and Management: Earthquake and Tsunami (प्राकृतिक आपदा एवं प्रबंधन : भूकंप एवं सुनामी)
  - Introduction: Focus (Hypocentre) and Epicentre of an Earthquake (विषय प्रवेश)
  - Seismic Zones of India (Zones II, III, IV, and V — North Bihar districts like Sitamarhi, Madhubani, Darbhanga, Supaul, Araria in high-risk Zone V) (भारत में भूकंप के क्षेत्र)
  - Seismic Waves: Primary (P-waves), Secondary (S-waves), and Surface/Long (L-waves) (भूकंपीय तरंग)
  - Measures for Protection Against Earthquakes: Earthquake-resistant building design (rectangular layout, pillar reinforcement) and safety drills (भूकंप से बचाव के उपाय)
  - Tsunami: Causes (submarine earthquakes) and 2004 Indian Ocean Tsunami
  - Measures for Protection Against Tsunami (सुनामी से बचाव के उपाय):
    * Early warning systems (INCOIS), Embankment construction, and Development of Coastal Mangrove Vegetation (तटबंध निर्माण तथा मैंग्रोव झाड़ी का विकास)
    * Training and Preparedness of People Living in Coastal Regions (तटीय प्रदेश के लोगों को प्रशिक्षण)

Chapter 4: Life-Saving Emergency / Contingency Management (जीवन रक्षक आकस्मिक प्रबंधन)
  - Distinction Between Long-Term Management and Immediate Emergency / Rescue Management
  - Emergency Management During Floods: Rescuing people/livestock to high ground, boats, life jackets, food packets, drinking water (halogen tablets), epidemic prevention (ORS, DDT/bleaching powder spraying) (बाढ़ की स्थिति में आकस्मिक प्रबंधन)
  - Emergency Management During Earthquakes and Tsunamis: Rescue from debris (Infra-red cameras, sniffer dogs, acoustic devices), first aid, relief camps, proper disposal of bodies (भूकंप एवं सुनामी की स्थिति में आकस्मिक प्रबंधन)
  - Emergency Management During Fire Outbreaks: Rescue of trapped persons, first aid for burns, cutting off electricity, use of sand, water, and foam/CO2 fire extinguishers (आग लगने की स्थिति में आकस्मिक प्रबंधन)
  - Essential First-Aid Equipment and Treatment for Rescue Workers (राहतकर्मियों के लिए प्राथमिक उपकरण और उपचार)
  - Key Components of Emergency Management: Local Administration, Community/Panchayat, and NGOs/NSS/NCC (आकस्मिक प्रबंधन के घटक)

Chapter 5: Alternative Communication Systems During Disasters (आपदा काल में वैकल्पिक संचार व्यवस्था)
  - Introduction: Breakdown of Regular Telecom/Power Networks During Major Disasters (विषय प्रवेश)
  - Alternative Communication Media (वैकल्पिक संचार साधन):
    * Radio Communication: Ultra High Frequency (UHF) and Very High Frequency (VHF) wireless walkie-talkies (रेडियो संचार)
    * Amateur or HAM Radio: Battery/generator operated non-satellite radio waves used by trained volunteers (एमेच्योर अथवा हैम रेडियो)
    * Satellite Communication: Transponders, Satellite Phones (SATPHONE), IRS (Indian Remote Sensing) and INSAT satellites for weather forecasting and remote disaster communication (उपग्रह संचार)

Chapter 6: Disasters and Co-existence (Living With Disasters) (आपदा और सह-अस्तित्व)
  - Concept of Co-existence with Natural Hazards by Adapting Lifestyle and Engineering
  - Practical Co-existence Strategies for Specific Disasters:
    * Earthquakes: Lightweight & seismic-resistant architecture (Examples from Japan) (भूकंप)
    * Landslides: Retaining walls, afforestation, avoiding steep slope excavation (भूस्खलन)
    * Tsunamis: Setback distance from coast, mangrove bioshields, concrete barriers (सुनामी)
    * Floods: Stilted/elevated houses, inflatable boats, flood-tolerant crop cycles (बाढ़)
    * Droughts: Watershed Management, Dryland Farming techniques, drip/sprinkler irrigation, drought-resistant seeds (सुखाड़, जल विभाजक क्षेत्र, शुष्क कृषि पद्धति)
  - Preparation of Village/Local Disaster Vulnerability Maps (आपदा मानचित्र)
  - Role of Non-Governmental (NGOs) and Semi-Governmental Organizations, Self-Help Groups, Youth Clubs, and Media in Disaster Resilience (गैर-सरकारी एवं अर्द्धसरकारी संगठन)
